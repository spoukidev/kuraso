# Governance

## Current Model

Kuraso starts under a **core-maintainer model** to keep v0.1 focused and fast. Once the project reaches ~10 regular contributors, governance will transition to a small **Steering Group** elected from active maintainers.

## Decision Making

- **Day-to-day PRs and bug fixes:** Reviewed and merged by maintainers.
- **Major architectural, file-format, or scope changes:** Proposed via a short RFC in `docs/rfcs/` with open community discussion before implementation.
- **Prioritization rule:** *"Does this help a student or researcher read and understand a paper?"*
