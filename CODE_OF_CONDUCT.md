# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone in the Kuraso community, regardless of background, language, academic level, or experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and kind in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Respect differing perspectives and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for students, researchers, and the open-source community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

Maintainers will enforce this Code of Conduct fairly and promptly.
